<h1>About Me</h1>

<p>
  Hi, this is Alexey Sedoykin, a binary personality:
  a man, an engineer, a husband, and a father.
</p>

<p>
  My experience is a symbiosis of deep understanding of both the design and operation of
  "big iron" classic heavy industrial machines that can be several stories high
  and hundreds of meters long and the software used to design and manage their product lifecycle.
</p>

<h2>Professional Journey</h2>

<p>
  I started working with industrial automation while still studying at university.
  After graduation, I remained at my alma mater and was immediately involved in implementing
  Teamcenter and Unigraphics (NX) into the educational process.
</p>

<p>
  Students solved real engineering and design tasks in NX, while their collaborative work was
  coordinated through Teamcenter. We organized the ability to work from anywhere in the world,
  and even evaluated student engagement, among other things, by their actual NX license usage.
</p>

<h2>2011–2022, 11 Years at Siemens Digital Industries Software</h2>

<p>
  I started as a Teamcenter trainer, and later spent 10 years working as a Local
  Product Manager for Teamcenter and related PLM solutions.
</p>

<p>
  During those years, I visited nearly all major industrial enterprises across Russia, from consumer
  goods manufacturers to aviation and rocket engineering plants. I also worked extensively in the
  countries of the former USSR and Turkey.
</p>

<p>
  My responsibilities included sales support, software implementation projects, solving complex
  technical challenges, consulting users, and collecting requirements for global development teams.
</p>

<p>
  Over the years, I accumulated a large number of ideas on how to improve software workflows,
  not only for Siemens Digital Industries Software solutions, but also for products from other
  vendors and even software outside the engineering domain.
</p>

<h2>Since 2022, Huawei</h2>

<p>
  For the first three years, I contributed as a Team Lead, Solution Architect, and Software Architect
  to the development of engineering software initiatives in the local Chinese market.
</p>

<p>
  One of the key projects that successfully evolved from a Proof of Concept into a fully-fledged
  product was a Digital Thread orchestration platform based on Graph Database, integrating data from
  heterogeneous engineering and enterprise information systems.
</p>

<p>
  Today, I am focused on building software infrastructure around hardware accelerators.
</p>
