# About Alexey Sedoykin

Alexey Sedoykin (Алексей Седойкин) is an engineer and software developer with a background in industrial automation, PLM, PDM, Teamcenter, NX, engineering software, Apple apps, and developer tools.

## Professional Journey

Alexey started working with industrial automation while studying at university. After graduation, he helped implement Teamcenter and Unigraphics (NX) into the educational process, including remote access workflows and analysis of real NX license usage.

## Siemens Digital Industries Software, 2011-2022

Alexey started as a Teamcenter trainer and later spent 10 years as a Local Product Manager for Teamcenter and related PLM solutions. His work included sales support, implementation projects, technical consulting, solving complex customer problems, and collecting requirements for global development teams.

## Huawei, Since 2022

Alexey worked as a Team Lead, Solution Architect, and Software Architect on engineering software initiatives, including a Digital Thread orchestration platform based on a graph database. He is now focused on software infrastructure around hardware accelerators.
